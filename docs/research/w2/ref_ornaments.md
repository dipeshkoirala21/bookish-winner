# Reference brief: roundabout centrepieces and island dressing (package **ornaments**)

> What a Kathmandu local sees at each roundabout today, measured from open-licensed photos (Wikimedia Commons, Flickr via Openverse) and OSM, written so the generators in `game/Assets/Ghumante/Core/Generators/Ornaments/` can be checked against it. Photos were viewed for reference only and are **not** in the repo (they live in `/home/user/wt/refs/ornaments/`). Facts and the full inventory are in [roundabouts.md](roundabouts.md); this file is the modelling brief: silhouette, proportions, colours, details, and the photo each one comes from. Branding rule (ASSET_MANIFEST): no logos, sponsor names or text; plaques are blank dark panels.
>
> Tags as in roundabouts.md: **[P]** read off a photo (credit in §9), **[O]** OSM / JNCT, **[S]** a written source, **[E]** an estimate or a design choice.

## 1. Shahid Gate (Martyrs' Memorial), Sundhara — `shahid_gate`

Photos: c_shahid 07 (Ksodari), 09 (Ekadeshmabysumirshrestha), 10, 11 (Anup Raj Rai), 16, 17 (Janak Bhatta), 08 aerial (Janak Bhatta).

* **Silhouette**: a wide flat "M" when seen from the road: low pavilion, huge pointed arch, low pavilion; the arch crown carries a lantern half as tall as the arch that makes the whole read like a stone bell-tower. Measured on c_07, c_10 and c_16 against the people on the steps (c_16, ≈ 1.7 m) and the 2.6 m plaque, as ratios of the width across the pavilions (≈ 22 m): clear span between the pavilions 0.40 W ≈ 9 m, pavilion width 0.28-0.30 W ≈ 6.6 m, pavilion peak 0.21 W ≈ 4.9 m above the island, intrados apex ≈ 7-7.5 m, extrados ≈ 9 m, lantern chamber ≈ 3.3 m tall and 4.8 m wide at its top, cap slab ≈ 6 m wide, peaked cap ≈ 1 m, mast top ≈ 15-16 m [P/E]. (The first pass guessed an 11 m span and a smaller lantern; the measured ratios replaced them.)
* **Arch**: pointed (two circular arcs meeting at the crown), legs **splay outward** towards the ground (the outer edge flares to ≈ 1.5× the leg width at the foot); section ≈ 1.6 m wide (in the gate's plane) × 1.4 m deep; the soffit and faces are **ashlar blocks** in two tones (beige `#CBB99D`, pale `#DCCFB8`) with grey joints `#8C8579`; a slightly projecting rounded edge on both faces [P].
* **Lantern**: two short legs continue the arch up; a pointed-arch opening (lancet) 1.6 m wide × 2.4 m high; inside on a small pedestal **King Tribhuvan's bust** (dark bronze `#3C3A2E`) with the plumed crown; above, a flat cap slab with slightly up-turned corners (a stylised pagoda roof) and a short finial with the double-pennant national flag (crimson `#DC143C`, blue border `#003893`) [P 07, 09].
* **Side pavilions**: ≈ 6.6 m wide × 4 m deep, walls ≈ 2.5 m above the platform, the gable rising to ≈ 4.9 m above the island: the gable faces the road, its edges gently concave with a **crested bead**, ending in **small horns turned up and out** at the eave corners (not a Chinese sweep), in the same stone; a tall **lancet niche** (≈ 1.9 × 2.7 m) on each face holds a **martyr's bust** (bronze) on a pedestal; the arch legs spring out of the pavilions' inner halves and **flare outward at the foot** [P 07, 10, 16].
* **Platform**: a stepped platform 0.9 m high (3 steps) between the pavilions, a broad flight of 6 steps under the arch towards the road; a dark granite plaque (blank) at the back of the platform [P 16].
* **Island**: the **round** park island (OSM w193705373, 30.3-30.9 m across in every direction, 30.5 m equal-area Ø; the first pass's 33 × 17 m stadium overshot it and left bare crescents) with a low stone kerb, a **black iron railing on a low cream plinth wall** with square **white stone gate posts with pyramid caps** at the entrance, red flower boxes on stands, trimmed hedges and lawn, solar street lights [P 07, 08].
* Respect: busts and Tribhuvan are single-colour bronze, no painted faces; no names on the plaque (ADR-005).

## 2. Maitighar Mandala — `maitighar`

Photos: c_maitighar 00, 01 (Ratopati.com, CC BY-SA 4.0), 02, 04-10 (flag, 2017-2019), Openverse maitighar2/00.

* **Mandala**: a square flat platform ≈ 24 m (OSM 24.6 m extent [O]), raised 0.3 m, its top a coloured inlay: outer square border **teal green** `#2E8B6E` with a pale yellow edge line `#F2E6A0`; four corner fields red `#C8323A`, blue `#2F5DA8`, yellow `#E8B923`, green; a big circle (Ø ≈ 21 m) of concentric bands: yellow `#F2C230`, orange `#E8742A`, red `#C8282E`, blue `#2D5DA8`, white `#F4EFE0`; a ring of **lotus petals** and a small central motif [P 00, 01; S Wikipedia: 32 vajras, 16 lotus petals, 32 garlands].
* **Marigold collar**: a thick ring of **yellow marigolds** `#F6C21C` hugs the square on all sides (a circle of flowers touching the square's corners) [P 00, 01].
* **Colonnade**: on the road side a row of slim **white columns with green tops** (≈ 10 columns, 3.2 m tall) carrying a light frame — a pergola fence [P 00, 01].
* **Flag**: since 2017 a **giant national flag** on a very tall steel pole (≈ 25-30 m; the flag ≈ 10 × 7.5 m [S]) at the island edge [P 04-10].
* **Perimeter**: **teal-green square fence posts with pyramid caps** (≈ 1.8 m) and rails round the island [P 07]; **solar street lights** (a tilted dark-blue panel on the pole) [P 02]; low red-brick planter walls `#A4553A` [P 00]; black-and-yellow painted kerb [P 00]; big lawns, clipped hedges, a small round raised bed with a feature [P 00].

## 3. Tripureshwor — `tripureshwor` (King Tribhuvan)

Photos: c_tribhuvan 00-02 (Janak Bhatta, CC BY-SA 4.0, at 27.69392, 85.31407).

* **Figure** ≈ 3.0 m, **white marble** `#ECEAE3`: long cloak from the shoulders to the ground (wide hem), knee-length coat, trousers, shoes; plumed crown (shirpech) with a short white plume; left hand at the waist holding the cloak/sword, right hand at the chest [P].
* **Garlands**: thick marigold garlands round the neck down to the knees: yellow `#F6C21C`, red `#C8233A`, white `#F5F3EA`, green leaf `#3E8E3A` [P] (offerings; a lovely real touch).
* **Pedestal**: square white die 1.6 m wide × 1.5 m with a moulded cornice and base, an engraved panel (blank) on the front; a 0.35 m socle under the feet [P].
* **Platform**: square, 3 broad steps (each 0.3 m high, 0.5 m deep) of grey stone `#B4AFA5`; big **terracotta pots** `#A0472E` (Ø 0.9 m) at the four corners and small pots on the steps [P 01, 02].
* Island 12.8 m [O]: lawn, hedge, solar street lights, painted kerb.

## 4. Jamal / Durbar Marg south — `durbar_marg` (King Mahendra)

Photos: c_mahendra 00, 02 (Janak Bhatta), 01 (Nirajan pant).

* **Figure** ≈ 3.0 m, **dark bronze** `#4A4836`: ceremonial uniform with a sash and medals, long cloak, plumed crown with a **tall curving plume**, **dark glasses**; one hand on the sword at the hip [P].
* **Pedestal**: tall **tapered die** (an obelisk stump) of cream-pink marble `#E4D3C2` with block joints, ≈ 1.5 m at the base to 1.2 m at the top, 3.2 m tall, a projecting cap slab; a **black bronze plaque** (blank) on the front; below it a wider square base (2.8 m, 1.0 m tall) with a moulded cornice and a **black granite plaque with a gilt border** (blank: no text, no sponsor) [P 01]; the base stands on **3 broad steps** of white stone and a low white parapet [P 00].
* Garden: roses and hedges, trees around, lamp posts [P/E].

## 5. New Road west, Basantapur — `new_road` (Juddha Shumsher)

Photos: c_juddha 00, 01 (Janak Bhatta), c_tribhuvan 04 (Ser Amantio di Nicolao), 05 (Stegop).

* **Figure** ≈ 2.8 m, **bronze** `#4D4B3A`: long cloak to the ground, Rana court uniform, the **tall curved plume** (bird-of-paradise) helmet, moustache, left hand on the sword hilt [P].
* **Pedestal**: pale grey stone `#D6D2C8`: a tall die ≈ 1.5 m wide × 3.2 m with inscription panels (blank) and a moulded cornice, crowned by a **round crest medallion** on the front, on a 0.6 m base [P 01, 05].
* **Platform**: **round, 4 steps** (Ø 9 m at the bottom), stone `#C9C3B6`, potted plants on the top step [P 00]; paved island, no lawn.

## 6. Jawalakhel — `jawalakhel` (King Birendra)

Photos: c_jawa 00-07, 12 (Janak Bhatta), 02 (Ser Amantio di Nicolao).

* **Spire**: a tall **concave-sided spire** (a flared obelisk: square-ish base ≈ 6 m flaring in, rising to a slim neck ≈ 0.9 m at ≈ 7 m), grey stone tiles `#9A968E` with lighter joints; at the top a **red and yellow collar** (`#C0392B`, `#E8B923`) under the statue [P 05].
* **Figure** ≈ 2.4 m in **green verdigris bronze** `#3D7A5C`: long robe and cloak, a big plume on the crown, glasses, right hand holding a sceptre upright [P 05 zoom]; often draped with red, white and green scarves [P].
* **Basin**: a round **sunken fountain basin** Ø ≈ 15 m around the spire: an outer cream stone rim `#E3DCCB`, inner steps down, a shallow pool, a **white tubular railing** round the inner edge, small **globe lamps** on short posts on the rim [P 05].
* **Garden** (island 35.9 m [O]): big lawn, flowing **marigold borders** (yellow and orange), round clipped golden shrubs, purple-flowered bushes, palms, conifers, agaves; **black-and-white kerb**; low **white ornamental railing** round the island; tall street lights [P 01, 12].

What the comparison changed in this pass: Shahid Gate was rescaled to the measured ratios (9 m span, taller pointed arch with a 1.6 m band, a lantern half the arch's height with a lancet opening, a 6 m cap slab and a low peaked cap; pavilions 6.6 m wide with gently concave crested gables and small horns instead of a Chinese sweep), stands on its round island and gets a mixed garden in front of and behind it; tall plants keep out of the centrepiece's sightlines; the moon on the flag is a crescent cradling a rayed disc.

Earlier passes: the arch legs splay at the foot (photo), the lantern became a bell chamber with a lancet, Tribhuvan's plume is short (photo) while Mahendra's, Birendra's and the Rana helmet's are tall and curved; the Jawalakhel spire got its strongly concave profile and red-yellow collar; Maitighar's marigold collar hugs the square and the colonnade stands on the road side; the flag of Nepal is drawn as parallel strip-cut layers so the crimson field and the emblems never vanish behind the blue border.
